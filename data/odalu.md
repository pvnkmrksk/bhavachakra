# ಒಡಲ ಚಕ್ರ
id: odalu
hub: ಒಡಲು | oḍalu · the body as vessel
tag: Seven seats of the body — not a translation of anything.

- ಎದೆ | ede | chest
  note: Nerve and collapse. Kannada puts courage here rather than in the heart — ಕೆಚ್ಚೆದೆ, a chest of embers — and puts the loss of it here too.
  - ಧೈರ್ಯ | dhairya | courage
    also: ಕೆಚ್ಚು | keccu | embers-courage ;; ಎದೆಗಾರಿಕೆ | edegārike | nerve ;; ದಿಟ್ಟತನ | diṭṭatana | boldness ;; ಛಲ | chala | resolve
    note: The chest holding.
    - ಕೆಚ್ಚೆದೆ | keccede | fierce courage
      lit: a chest of embers
      note: ಕೆಚ್ಚು is heat held in the body. The bravery word is thermal, not moral.
    - ಎದೆಗಾರಿಕೆ | edegārike | nerve
      lit: chest-having
      note: The willingness to stand up and say it — closer to 'having the guts', one organ higher.
  - ಕುಗ್ಗು | kuggu | losing heart
    note: The English wheel repeats <i>inferior</i> in two branches. Kannada usefully does not: under fear it is ಕೀಳರಿಮೆ, a belief about your rank; here under sadness it is simply shrinking.
    - ಎದೆಗುಂದು | edegundu | dismay
      lit: the chest sinks
      note: The standard word. Kannada names the physical drop and leaves the feeling to be inferred.
    - ಎದೆ ಒಡೆ | ede oḍe | devastation
      lit: the chest breaks
      note: Reserved for news that arrives all at once. English 'heartbreak' drifted toward romance; this did not.
  - ದಿಗಿಲು | digilu | dread
    also: ಆತಂಕ | ātaṅka | anxiety ;; ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ಭೀತಿ | bhīti | terror
    note: The chest reacting before you do.
    - ಎದೆ ಡವಡವ | ede ḍavaḍava | thudding fear
      lit: the chest goes thud-thud
      note: Anticipatory — the fear of a thing you can see coming.
    - ಎದೆ ಝಲ್ | ede jhal | the jolt of alarm
      lit: the chest goes cold, once
      note: A single event, not a state. There is no English noun for the one jolt.
  - ಹೆಮ್ಮೆ | hemme | pride
    also: ಅಭಿಮಾನ | abhimāna | pride as loyalty ;; ಗರ್ವ | garva | pride, tipping toward vanity ;; ಅಹಂಕಾರ | ahaṅkāra | the pride that has gone bad ;; ಗತ್ತು | gattu | swagger
    note: The chest filling.
    - ಎದೆ ತುಂಬು | ede tumbu | pride on someone's behalf
      lit: the chest fills
      note: Said watching someone you raised do well. Not self-pride — English needs a whole clause.
    - ಎದೆಯುಬ್ಬು | edeyubbu | swelling pride
      lit: the chest swells
      note: One degree louder, and faintly comic if you use it about yourself.
  - ಸಂಕಟ | saṅkaṭa | anguish
    also: ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ಒದ್ದಾಟ | oddāṭa | thrashing about ;; ಪರದಾಟ | paradāṭa | floundering
    note: The chest closing.
    - ಎದೆ ಭಾರ | ede bhāra | the weight before weeping
      lit: the chest is heavy
      note: The state just before tears, named as a load rather than a mood.
    - ಉಸಿರುಗಟ್ಟು | usirugaṭṭu | suffocation
      lit: the breath is blocked
      note: Used for a room, a marriage and a job. Nobody hears it as metaphor.
- ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ | hoṭṭe | belly
  note: Appetite and envy. Kannada is unembarrassed about siting the ugly feelings in the stomach, and says them out loud.
  - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಕಿಚ್ಚು | hoṭṭe-kiccu | envy
    also: ಅಸೂಯೆ | asūye | envy ;; ಮಾತ್ಸರ್ಯ | mātsarya | envious rivalry ;; ಕರುಬು | karubu | to begrudge, to smoulder ;; ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯುರಿ | hoṭṭeyuri | the burn of being wronged
    note: Belly-fire. Envy located precisely in the stomach, and said out loud — everyone knows which organ is burning.
    - ಅಸೂಯೆ | asūye | envy, formally
      note: Envy named directly, without the belly. Used in writing and in speech alike.
    - ಕರುಬು | karubu | to begrudge
      lit: to smoulder
      note: Native verb. The low-grade continuous version of the same fire.
  - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯುರಿ | hoṭṭeyuri | burning resentment
    note: Distinct from envy: the heat of having been wronged, not of wanting what another has.
    - ಅಸಮಾಧಾನ | asamādhāna | discontent
      note: 'Un-settledness' — a grievance nobody talked down.
    - ಸೇಡು | sēḍu | revenge
      note: ಸೇಡು ತೀರಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳು — to settle it — treats revenge as a debt.
  - ತೃಪ್ತಿ | tṛpti | satiety
    also: ಸಂತೃಪ್ತಿ | santṛpti | full satisfaction ;; ತಣಿವು | taṇivu | slaked ;; ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | settledness
    note: The belly at rest.
    - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ ತುಂಬು | hoṭṭe tumbu | enough
      lit: the belly is full
      note: Used far past food: a full belly is the standard image for having had sufficient of anything.
    - ತಣಿವು | taṇivu | slaked
      lit: cooling
      note: Satisfaction as cooling rather than filling — the other half of how Kannada thinks about want.
  - ದುರಾಸೆ | durāse | greed
    note: ಆಸೆ is desire and is morally neutral; the prefix does all the work.
    - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಬಾಕ | hoṭṭe-bāka | glutton
      lit: belly-eater
      note: An insult about appetite that transfers cleanly to money and power.
    - ಆಸೆಬುರುಕ | āseburuka | grasping
      lit: full of wanting
      note: The -ಬುರುಕ suffix makes any noun into a person overfull of it.
  - ವಾಕರಿಕೆ | vākarike | revulsion
    note: The belly rejecting.
    - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ ತೊಳಸು | hoṭṭe toḷasu | the stomach stirs
      lit: the stomach is stirred
      note: Moral disgust and physical nausea in one phrase, with no marker between them.
    - ಹೇಸಿಗೆ | hēsige | loathing
      lit: filth
      also: ಅಸಹ್ಯ | asahya | disgust ;; ಕೊಳಕು | koḷaku | dirt ;; ಜುಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jugupse | revulsion
      note: The moral and the physical are the same word.
- ಕರುಳು | karuḷu | gut
  note: The most untranslatable seat. In Kannada the gut is the organ of kinship — your child is your ಕರುಳ ಬಳ್ಳಿ, your gut-vine — so every feeling sited here is about your own people.
  - ಕನಿಕರ | kanikara | compassion
    also: ಅನುಕಂಪ | anukampa | fellow-feeling ;; ಮರುಕ | maruka | pity, ruth ;; ಕರುಣೆ | karuṇe | mercy ;; ದಯೆ | daye | kindness
    note: What the gut does when it sees suffering.
    - ಕರುಳು ಚುರುಕ್ | karuḷu curuk | the pang of pity
      lit: the gut stings
      note: Involuntary, on seeing a child or an animal in distress. Pity is a judgement; this is a reflex.
    - ಮರುಕ | maruka | pity, ruth
      lit: turning back toward
      note: 'Turning back toward' — pity as the movement of turning to look again at someone.
  - ಮರುಗು | maragu | grieving for another
    note: ಮರುಗು is a native verb with no exact English partner: to ache on someone else's account. The gut under strain.
    - ಕರುಳು ಹಿಂಡು | karuḷu hiṇḍu | wrung with pity
      lit: the gut is wrung
      note: The image is wringing a wet cloth. Used for watching suffering you cannot stop.
    - ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn
      note: The word for boiling liquid and for a mind that will not settle.
  - ಅಗಲಿಕೆ | agalike | loss, parting
    note: The gut torn.
    - ಕರುಳು ಕಿತ್ತು ಬರು | karuḷu kittu baru | losing your own
      lit: the gut tears loose
      note: Kept for the death of a child or parent. Using it lightly would be shocking.
    - ಉಮ್ಮಳ | ummaḷa | grief welling up
      lit: a swelling from below
      note: Names the swell, not the weeping.
  - ಮಮತೆ | mamate | attachment-love
    note: The gut as a tie.
    - ಕರುಳ ಬಳ್ಳಿ | karuḷa baḷḷi | one's own child
      lit: the gut-vine
      note: The umbilical cord as a creeper. English has no everyday phrase saying kinship is physical.
    - ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ | vātsalya | downward tenderness
      lit: the feeling toward a calf
      also: ಮಮತೆ | mamate | attachment-love ;; ಅಕ್ಕರೆ | akkare | fondness ;; ಕಕ್ಕುಲತೆ | kakkulate | anxious tenderness
      note: Flows one way only: elder to younger. English 'love' has no direction; this word is nothing but direction.
- ಮನಸ್ಸು | manassu | mind
  note: The general seat, and the only one that is not an organ you could point to. Everything a body does, the ಮನಸ್ಸು does — it fills, shrinks, lightens, opens.
  - ನೋವು | nōvu | hurt
    also: ಬೇನೆ | bēne | ache, ailment ;; ಯಾತನೆ | yātane | torment ;; ಬಾಧೆ | bādhe | affliction
    note: ನೋವು is bodily and emotional pain with no distinction at all.
    - ಮನಸ್ಸಿಗೆ ನೋವು | manassige nōvu | being wounded
      lit: pain to the mind
      note: The ordinary way to say someone hurt you.
    - ಮನಸ್ತಾಪ | manastāpa | a falling-out
      lit: mind-heat
      note: Between two people who were close. Not anger, not grief — the cooled residue that keeps them apart.
  - ಖಿನ್ನತೆ | khinnate | lowness
    note: rala's first hits for <i>depressed</i> are ದಲಿತ and ಶೋಷಿತ — from the colonial administrative phrase 'depressed classes'. ಖಿನ್ನತೆ is the clinical word; ಮನಸ್ಸು ಕುಗ್ಗಿದೆ, the mind has shrunk, is the older way of saying it.
    - ಮನಸ್ಸು ಕುಗ್ಗು | manassu kuggu | the mind shrinks
      lit: the mind shrinks down
      note: What Kannada said before ಖಿನ್ನತೆ was coined. A description, where the new word is a diagnosis.
    - ಬೇಸರ | bēsara | weary discontent
      also: ಬೇಜಾರು | bējāru | fed up, the same word one register down ;; ಸಪ್ಪೆ | sappe | flat, unsalted ;; ಜಡ | jaḍa | inert
      note: One word for bored, mildly sad and fed up. 'ಬೇಸರಾಗಿದೆ' could be any of the three.
  - ತೆರೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳು | teredukoḷḷu | opening up
    note: The nearest Kannada gets to the therapeutic sense of vulnerable — and it is an act, not a state.
    - ಮನಸ್ಸು ಬಿಚ್ಚು | manassu biccu | to unfold the mind
      lit: to untie the mind
      note: The same verb as untying a knot or opening a parcel.
    - ಸಲಿಗೆ | salige | earned informality
      also: ಆತ್ಮೀಯತೆ | ātmīyate | closeness ;; ಅನ್ಯೋನ್ಯ | anyōnya | mutual, easy with each other ;; ನಿಕಟ | nikaṭa | near
      note: The licence to tease someone, eat off their plate, drop the honorific. Intimacy defined as permission.
  - ಕರಗು | karagu | being moved
    note: The mind melting — Kannada's standard image for being touched.
    - ಮನ ಮುಟ್ಟು | mana muṭṭu | it touched me
      lit: it touched the mind
      note: The agent is the thing, not you. A song ಮನ ಮುಟ್ಟುತ್ತದೆ.
    - ಮೆಚ್ಚುಗೆ | meccuge | admiration
      also: ಪ್ರಶಂಸೆ | praśaṁse | praise ;; ಒಲವು | olavu | leaning toward ;; ಇಷ್ಟ | iṣṭa | liking
      note: Both the feeling and its expression — to ಮೆಚ್ಚು silently is incomplete.
  - ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ | nemmadi | peace of mind
    also: ಶಾಂತಿ | śānti | peace, as the absence of conflict ;; ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | being consoled ;; ನಿರಾಳ | nirāḷa | unclenched
    note: Sharply distinct from ಶಾಂತಿ, peace as the absence of conflict.
    - ಮನಸ್ಸು ಹಗುರ | manassu hagura | relief
      lit: the mind is light
      note: Specifically after confessing or weeping. Relief as a change in weight.
    - ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | being consoled
      lit: a settling
      also: ಸಂತೈಕೆ | santaike | consoling ;; ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ | nemmadi | peace of mind ;; ತಣ್ಣಗಾಗು | taṇṇagāgu | to cool down
      note: Both the comfort someone gives you and the state it produces.
  - ಒಪ್ಪಿಗೆ | oppige | assent
    note: Kannada distinguishes agreeing out loud from your ಮನಸ್ಸು having agreed, and gives you the phrase to say so.
    - ಮನಸ್ಸು ಒಪ್ಪು | manassu oppu | felt consent
      lit: the mind agrees
      note: You can say yes without this having happened, and everyone knows it.
    - ಮನಃಪೂರ್ವಕ | manaḥpūrvaka | wholeheartedly
      lit: mind-first
      note: The adverb you attach to a thank-you to mean you meant it.
- ತಲೆ | tale | head
  note: Worry and standing. The head overheats, spoils and spins — and it is also the thing you raise or lower in front of other people.
  - ಚಿಂತೆ | cinte | worry
    also: ಯೋಚನೆ | yōcane | thinking it over ;; ಕಾಳಜಿ | kāḷaji | care, concern ;; ತಲೆಬಿಸಿ | talebisi | head-heat
    note: ಚಿಂತೆ is also simply 'thought'. To worry and to think are the same word.
    - ತಲೆಬಿಸಿ | talebisi | worry as overheating
      lit: head-heat
      note: You can tell someone not to take ತಲೆಬಿಸಿ the way you would tell them to cool down.
    - ಯೋಚನೆ | yōcane | thinking it over
      note: Neutral by itself; 'ಯೋಚನೆ ಮಾಡಬೇಡ' means stop worrying.
  - ಗೊಂದಲ | gondala | confusion
    also: ಕಕ್ಕಾಬಿಕ್ಕಿ | kakkābikki | flustered ;; ತಬ್ಬಿಬ್ಬು | tabbibbu | thrown, at a loss ;; ಗಲಿಬಿಲಿ | galibili | muddle
    note: Also the word for a noisy crowd — confusion as too many voices at once.
    - ತಲೆಕೆಡು | talekeḍu | driven out of your mind
      lit: the head spoils
      note: Same verb as milk going off. Covers exam season and genuine breakdown, with tone doing the work.
    - ಕಕ್ಕಾಬಿಕ್ಕಿ | kakkābikki | flustered
      note: A sound-word with no parts that mean anything alone — the flap of not knowing what to do with your hands.
  - ಹೈರಾಣ | hairāṇa | worn out
    note: Overwhelm as depletion rather than dread.
    - ತಲೆ ಸುತ್ತು | tale suttu | can't take any more
      lit: the head spins
      note: Physical dizziness and being overwhelmed share the phrase entirely.
    - ದಣಿವು | daṇivu | tiredness
      also: ಆಯಾಸ | āyāsa | fatigue ;; ಬಳಲಿಕೆ | baḷalike | exhaustion ;; ಸುಸ್ತು | sustu | done in
      note: The only match rala had for <i>tired</i> was agronomy — ದಣಿದ ಮಣ್ಣು, exhausted soil. ದಣಿವು is used of a body and of a day alike.
  - ಮರ್ಯಾದೆ | maryāde | standing, face
    note: One of the most-used words in Kannada. Self-respect described entirely as a posture held in public.
    - ತಲೆ ಎತ್ತು | tale ettu | dignity
      lit: to raise the head
      note: Dignity described as a posture: the head carried level in front of other people.
    - ತಲೆತಗ್ಗಿಸು | taletaggisu | shame
      lit: to lower the head
      note: The same axis in the other direction. Kannada's shame is visible before it is internal.
- ಮೈ | mai | body
  note: The surface. Everything here is involuntary and visible — the hair standing up, the burn, the forgetting of the body altogether.
  - ಪುಳಕ | puḷaka | thrill
    note: Classical poetics counts it as visible evidence of an inner state, which is why it has its own noun.
    - ಮೈ ಝುಮ್ | mai jhum | the thrill of music
      lit: the body tingles
      note: Specifically aesthetic. You would say it about a raga, rarely about good news.
    - ರೋಮಾಂಚ | rōmāñca | horripilation
      lit: hair-motion
      note: The Sanskrit register of exactly the same event.
  - ನಡುಕ | naḍuka | the body's alarm
    note: The skin knowing before the mind does. Kannada names the tremble and lets the fear be inferred.
    - ಮೈ ನವಿರೇಳು | mai navirēḷu | awe or dread
      lit: the fine hair stands
      note: Which of the two it is comes from context alone. Kannada declines to separate the physiology.
    - ಬೆವರು | bevaru | sweat
      note: 'ಬೆವರಿಬಿಟ್ಟೆ' — I broke into a sweat — is a complete statement about fear.
  - ಉರಿ | uri | burning
    note: The point at which anger stops being an opinion.
    - ಮೈ ಉರಿ | mai uri | rage gone physical
      lit: the body burns
      note: Also literally a fever or a rash — the phrase does not choose.
    - ಸಿಡುಕು | siḍuku | worn-in irritability
      note: Not an episode but a temperament, and one you wear on your face.
  - ಮೈಮರೆವು | maimarevu | absorption
    note: The body forgotten — the highest praise available for listening to music.
    - ಮೈ ಮರೆ | mai mare | lost in it
      lit: to forget the body
      note: Also what you say about someone who missed their stop.
    - ಭಾವಪರವಶ | bhāva-paravaśa | carried away
      lit: subject to the feeling
      note: ಪರವಶ means under another's control. The grammar says the feeling is driving.
- ಮುಖ | mukha | face
  note: The public instrument. These are the feelings other people read off you whether or not you meant them to — which is why the sulk lives here and not in ಕೋಪ.
  - ಮುನಿಸು | munisu | the loving sulk
    also: ಸೆಡವು | seḍavu | a huff ;; ಕೋಪ | kōpa | anger ;; ಬಿಗುಮಾನ | bigumāna | held stiffness
    note: The anger you are only entitled to with someone who loves you. It wants soothing, not resolution, and would be insulted by an apology that was merely correct.
    - ಮುಖ ಊದಿಸು | mukha ūdisu | sulking
      lit: to puff the face
      note: Described as something you actively do to your own face. The performance is the point.
    - ಸೆಡವು | seḍavu | a huff
      note: Native, and shorter-lived than ಮುನಿಸು — an afternoon rather than a week.
  - ಸಪ್ಪೆ | sappe | flatness
    note: ಸಪ್ಪೆ is what you call unsalted food. A dejected face is described as under-seasoned.
    - ಮುಖ ಕಪ್ಪಿಡು | mukha kappiḍu | crestfallen
      lit: the face darkens
      note: The visible fall when someone hears they were left out.
    - ಸಪ್ಪೆ ಮೋರೆ | sappe mōre | a long face
      lit: an insipid face
      note: ಮೋರೆ is the blunt, slightly rude word for a face.
  - ಅಳು | aḷu | weeping
    note: The plainest native verb. The eye as the place the mind overflows.
    - ಕಣ್ಣು ತುಂಬು | kaṇṇu tumbu | moved to tears
      lit: the eyes fill
      note: Reversible in a way English is not: a sight can also ಕಣ್ಣು ತುಂಬು you, by being beautiful.
    - ಕಣ್ಣೀರು | kaṇṇīru | tears
      lit: eye-water
      note: Plain compound, no ceremony. Kannada saves its ceremony for the phrases around it.
  - ಸಿಟ್ಟು | siṭṭu | visible anger
    also: ಕೋಪ | kōpa | anger, composed ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ಮುನಿಸು | munisu | the loving sulk ;; ಸೆಡವು | seḍavu | a huff
    note: Hot and quick, and the most-used of the anger words.
    - ಕಣ್ಣು ಕೆಂಪು | kaṇṇu kempu | about to break
      lit: red eyes
      note: A warning read by everyone present. The threat is in the description, not in any word for anger.
    - ಹುಬ್ಬು ಗಂಟು | hubbu gaṇṭu | a knitted brow
      lit: the eyebrows knot
      note: The smallest visible unit of displeasure, and often the only one you get.
  - ನಾಚಿಕೆ | nāchike | shyness and shame at once
    also: ಸಂಕೋಚ | saṅkōca | shrinking, reticence ;; ಮುಜುಗರ | mujugara | awkwardness ;; ಲಜ್ಜೆ | lajje | modesty, shame
    note: A bride's ನಾಚಿಕೆ and a thief's are the same noun. English needs three words and grades them differently; Kannada trusts context completely.
    - ಕಣ್ಣು ತಗ್ಗಿಸು | kaṇṇu taggisu | averting the eyes
      lit: to lower the eyes
      note: The gesture that covers modesty, shyness and guilt without distinguishing them.
    - ಮುಜುಗರ | mujugara | awkwardness
      also: ಸಂಕೋಚ | saṅkōca | shrinking ;; ಇರುಸುಮುರುಸು | irusumurusu | squirming discomfort ;; ಕಸಿವಿಸಿ | kasivisi | small unease
      note: The wince at a social scene, often on someone else's behalf.
