# ಭಾವಚಕ್ರ
id: bhava
hub: ಭಾವಚಕ್ರ | bhāva-cakra
tag: Seven core feelings, three rings — the English feeling wheel put into Kannada, seams and all.

- ಸಂತೋಷ | santōṣa | Happy
  also: ಖುಷಿ | khuṣi | gladness, the most casual ;; ಹರ್ಷ | harṣa | joy, elevated ;; ಆನಂದ | ānanda | bliss ;; ಸಂತಸ | santasa | gladness, softer ;; ಹಿಗ್ಗು | higgu | swelling delight
  note: rala also returns ಭಾಗ್ಯವಂತ / ಅದೃಷ್ಟಶಾಲಿ — 'lucky'. English <i>happy</i> still carries its old root <i>hap</i>, chance. Kannada keeps luck and gladness in separate words.
  - ತುಂಟತನ | ṭuṇṭatana | Playful
    note: A clean miss. rala only knows <i>play</i> as the noun — foul play, playwright, child's play. The felt state is ತುಂಟತನ, the mischief of a child you are not actually angry with.
    - ಉದ್ರೇಕ | udrēka | Aroused
      note: In Kannada ಉದ್ರೇಕ is not primarily erotic — a crowd, a temper and a nerve can all be ಉದ್ರಿಕ್ತ. It means charged, and the charge can go either way.
    - ಕೀಟಲೆ | kīṭale | Cheeky
      note: rala's ಉದ್ಧಟ / ದುರಹಂಕಾರ are genuinely insulting. Cheeky is affectionate — that is ಕೀಟಲೆ, teasing you are allowed to do.
  - ತೃಪ್ತಿ | tṛpti | Content
    also: ಸಂತೃಪ್ತಿ | santṛpti | full satisfaction ;; ತಣಿವು | taṇivu | slaked ;; ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | settledness
    note: The right word was in there, sitting under moisture content and table of contents. ತೃಪ್ತಿ is satiety — the feeling after a meal, and after a life.
    - ನಿರಾಳ | nirāḷa | Free
      note: ಮುಕ್ತ and ಸ್ವತಂತ್ರ are freedoms of status — liberated, independent, tax-exempt. The <i>feeling</i> of free is ನಿರಾಳ: unclenched, the breath after the weight comes off.
    - ಹಿಗ್ಗು | higgu | Joyful
      note: To swell. Joy as something that expands you — the same verb for dough rising and for a chest filling.
  - ಆಸಕ್ತಿ | āsakti | Interested
    - ಕುತೂಹಲ | kutūhala | Curious
    - ಕೆದಕು | kedaku | Inquisitive
      also: ಜಿಜ್ಞಾಸೆ | jijñāse | the wish to know ;; ಕುತೂಹಲ | kutūhala | curiosity ;; ಶೋಧ | śōdha | searching out
      note: ಕೆದಕು is to poke at a thing that was sitting quietly, and it keeps the faint rudeness English <i>inquisitive</i> also carries. ಜಿಜ್ಞಾಸೆ is the same impulse pointed at ideas rather than at people.
  - ಹೆಮ್ಮೆ | hemme | Proud
    also: ಅಭಿಮಾನ | abhimāna | pride as loyalty ;; ಗರ್ವ | garva | pride, tipping toward vanity ;; ಅಹಂಕಾರ | ahaṅkāra | the pride that has gone bad ;; ಗತ್ತು | gattu | swagger
    note: rala returns nothing at all for <i>proud</i>. And Kannada would resist a single answer anyway: ಹೆಮ್ಮೆ is warm pride in someone, ಅಭಿಮಾನ is pride-as-loyalty, ಅಹಂಕಾರ is the pride that has gone bad. English collapses all three.
    - ಸಾರ್ಥಕ | sārthaka | Successful
      note: ಯಶಸ್ವಿ is the outcome — you won. ಸಾರ್ಥಕ is the feeling — it had meaning, it was worth it. Only one of those belongs on an emotion wheel.
    - ಆತ್ಮವಿಶ್ವಾಸ | ātma-viśvāsa | Confident
      note: Literally 'self-trust'. Kannada builds confidence out of the same root as trusting another person.
  - ಒಪ್ಪಿಗೆ | oppige | Accepted
    note: Every hit is procurement paperwork. And Kannada has no noun for <i>the felt state of being accepted</i> — you say it as something others did: ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಒಪ್ಪಿಕೊಂಡರು, 'they took me in'. The feeling lives in a verb, not a noun.
    - ಗೌರವ | gaurava | Respected
      note: rala only found the clerical <i>in respect of</i>. ಗೌರವ is the real word, and in Kannada it is something you give, actively, not something you passively have.
    - ಮನ್ನಣೆ | mannaṇe | Valued
      note: ಮೌಲ್ಯ is price. ಮನ್ನಣೆ is being recognised and given your due — the thing people leave jobs for the lack of.
  - ಶಕ್ತಿ | śakti | Powerful
    - ಧೈರ್ಯ | dhairya | Courageous
      also: ಕೆಚ್ಚು | keccu | embers-courage ;; ಎದೆಗಾರಿಕೆ | edegārike | nerve ;; ದಿಟ್ಟತನ | diṭṭatana | boldness ;; ಛಲ | chala | resolve
      note: ಧೈರ್ಯ is steadiness under fear. The native alternatives rala offers are more physical: ಕೆಚ್ಚು is heat in the chest, ಎದೆಗಾರಿಕೆ is literally chest-having.
    - ಹೊಳಹು | hoḷahu | Creative
      also: ಸೃಜನಶೀಲತೆ | sṛjanaśīlate | creativity ;; ಕಲ್ಪನೆ | kalpane | imagining ;; ಸ್ಫೂರ್ತಿ | sphūrti | inspiration
      note: The flash — the moment a thing occurs to you. Names the event rather than the faculty.
  - ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ | nemmadi | Peaceful
    also: ಶಾಂತಿ | śānti | peace, as the absence of conflict ;; ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | being consoled ;; ನಿರಾಳ | nirāḷa | unclenched
    note: The single most important correction on this wheel. ಶಾಂತಿ is peace as the absence of war — treaties, ceasefires, ಶಾಂತಿ ಸಭೆ. ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ is peace of mind, and it is what people actually pray for.
    - ಪ್ರೀತಿ | prīti | Loving
      also: ಮಮತೆ | mamate | attachment-love ;; ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ | vātsalya | tenderness flowing downward ;; ಅಕ್ಕರೆ | akkare | fondness ;; ಒಲವು | olavu | leaning toward
      note: The only match in 478,680 entries was a botany term for shade-loving plants. Kannada is not short of love words — ಪ್ರೀತಿ, ಮಮತೆ, ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ, ಅಕ್ಕರೆ, ಒಲವು — the dictionary just isn't built to find them from English.
    - ಕೃತಜ್ಞತೆ | kṛtajñate | Thankful
      note: Literally 'knowing what was done'. Gratitude as accurate memory.
  - ನಂಬಿಕೆ | nambike | Trusting
    note: Buried under thirty entries of trust deeds and trust accounts. ನಂಬಿಕೆ also means belief and superstition — in Kannada, trusting a person and believing a thing are one act.
    - ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮ | sūkṣma | Sensitive
      note: ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮ means fine-grained, subtle-perceiving. Calling someone ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮ is praise — unlike English 'sensitive', which is half an accusation.
    - ಸಲಿಗೆ | salige | Intimate
      also: ಆತ್ಮೀಯತೆ | ātmīyate | closeness ;; ಅನ್ಯೋನ್ಯ | anyōnya | mutual, easy with each other ;; ನಿಕಟ | nikaṭa | near
      note: ಸಲಿಗೆ has no English word. It is the earned licence to be informal with someone — to tease them, take their food, drop the honorific. Intimacy defined as permission, not as feeling.
  - ಭರವಸೆ | bharavase | Optimistic
    also: ಆಶಾವಾದ | āśāvāda | optimism, as a stance ;; ನಿರೀಕ್ಷೆ | nirīkṣe | expectation ;; ನಂಬಿಕೆ | nambike | trust, belief
    note: ಭರವಸೆ is also the word for a promise or an assurance. Optimism, in Kannada, is something somebody gave you.
    - ಆಸೆ | āse | Hopeful
      also: ಆಶೆ | āśe | hope, the Sanskrit form ;; ಹಂಬಲ | hambala | yearning ;; ಬಯಕೆ | bayake | wish
      note: The plainest possible word: wish, want, hope, all one. Kannada does not separate hoping from wanting.
    - ಸ್ಫೂರ್ತಿ | sphūrti | Inspired
      note: No result. ಸ್ಫೂರ್ತಿ is the everyday word — a sudden welling-up, the same root as a spark.
- ಅಚ್ಚರಿ | accari | Surprised
  also: ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ | āścarya | surprise, the Sanskrit form ;; ವಿಸ್ಮಯ | vismaya | astonishment ;; ಬೆರಗು | beragu | wonder that stops you
  note: ಅಚ್ಚರಿ is the native word and ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ the Sanskrit one; both are in daily use, and this wheel uses the shorter.
  - ಬೆಚ್ಚು | beccu | Startled
    note: ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು — to be startled and drop. Kannada builds the flinch out of a fall.
    - ಆಘಾತ | āghāta | Shocked
    - ಎದೆಗುಂದು | edegundu | Dismayed
      note: Literally 'the chest sinks'. Kannada names the physical event and leaves you to infer the feeling — it does this constantly.
  - ಗೊಂದಲ | gondala | Confused
    also: ಕಕ್ಕಾಬಿಕ್ಕಿ | kakkābikki | flustered ;; ತಬ್ಬಿಬ್ಬು | tabbibbu | thrown, at a loss ;; ಗಲಿಬಿಲಿ | galibili | muddle
    note: The dictionary's one match for <i>confused</i> is a species of beetle. ಗೊಂದಲ is the real word, and it also means a noisy crowd — confusion as too many voices at once.
    - ಭ್ರಮೆ ಕಳಚು | bhrame kaḷacu | Disillusioned
      note: The illusion comes unfastened. ಕಳಚು is what a bangle does, or a bolt.
    - ಕಂಗೆಡು | kaṅgeḍu | Perplexed
      note: ಕಣ್ + ಕೆಡು: the eyes go bad. To be at a loss is, literally, to lose your sight of it.
  - ಬೆರಗು | beragu | Amazed
    also: ವಿಸ್ಮಯ | vismaya | astonishment ;; ಅಚ್ಚರಿ | accari | surprise ;; ಸೋಜಿಗ | sōjiga | marvel
    - ದಂಗು | daṅgu | Astonished
      also: ವಿಸ್ಮಯ | vismaya | astonishment ;; ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ | āścarya | surprise ;; ಬೆರಗು | beragu | wonder ;; ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ | digbhrame | stupefaction
      note: ದಂಗಾದೆ — I was floored. The version of astonishment with your mouth open.
    - ಭಯಭಕ್ತಿ | bhaya-bhakti | Awe
      note: rala could only define it as a phrase: 'respect mixed with fear'. But Kannada does have the compound — ಭಯಭಕ್ತಿ, fear-and-devotion, the standard word for how one stands before a deity or a formidable elder. Awe as a social posture, not a private thrill.
  - ಉತ್ಸಾಹ | utsāha | Excited
    also: ಹುರುಪು | hurupu | vigour ;; ಉಮೇದು | umēdu | zest ;; ಹುಮ್ಮಸ್ಸು | hummassu | drive ;; ಸಡಗರ | saḍagara | bustle and delight
    - ತವಕ | tavaka | Eager
      note: ತವಕ and ಕಾತರ are both eagerness with an edge of ache — waiting that has begun to hurt slightly.
    - ಹುರುಪು | hurupu | Energetic
- ಬೇಸರ | bēsara | Bad
  also: ಬೇಜಾರು | bējāru | fed up, the same word one register down ;; ಸಪ್ಪೆ | sappe | flat, unsalted ;; ಜಡ | jaḍa | inert
  note: The hardest sector. Kannada's ಕೆಟ್ಟ is moral or qualitative — a bad man, spoiled milk — and cannot be a feeling. But look at what the English wheel actually files under 'Bad': bored, busy, stressed, tired. That whole zone has one Kannada name, ಬೇಸರ — a fused weariness-with-things that English needs four words to circle.
  - ಬೇಜಾರು | bējāru | Bored
    also: ಬೇಸರ | bēsara | weary discontent ;; ಜಿಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jigupse | revulsion, world-weariness ;; ಸಪ್ಪೆ | sappe | flat
    note: No entry for <i>boredom</i>. ಬೇಜಾರು covers bored, mildly sad, and fed-up in one breath. 'ಬೇಜಾರಾಗಿದೆ' could be any of the three and the listener works it out from your face.
    - ಉದಾಸೀನ | udāsīna | Indifferent
      note: In philosophy ಉದಾಸೀನ is the sage's equanimity. In an argument it is the coldest insult available.
    - ಅಸಡ್ಡೆ | asaḍḍe | Apathetic
      also: ನಿರಾಸಕ್ತಿ | nirāsakti | disinterest ;; ಉದಾಸೀನ | udāsīna | indifference, or the cold shoulder ;; ತಾತ್ಸಾರ | tātsāra | disdain
      note: Not caring, and not quite bothering to hide that you are not caring.
  - ಧಾವಂತ | dhāvanta | Busy
    note: rala's words describe a schedule. ಧಾವಂತ describes what the schedule does to you — the harried forward-lean of someone always mid-errand.
    - ಒತ್ತಡ | ottaḍa | Pressured
      note: Same word for atmospheric pressure, blood pressure, and social pressure. Kannada did not borrow 'stress' — it extended 'push'.
    - ಆತುರ | ātura | Rushed
      note: ಆತುರ is haste as a character flaw as much as a state — 'ಆತುರಗಾರನಿಗೆ ಬುದ್ಧಿ ಮಟ್ಟ', the hasty man is short on sense.
  - ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | Stressed
    note: Every single hit was materials engineering. ತಳಮಳ is the churn — the word for boiling liquid and for a mind that will not settle.
    - ಹೈರಾಣ | hairāṇa | Overwhelmed
      note: English uses one <i>overwhelmed</i> in two places on this wheel. Kannada splits them by how you are swamped: ಹೈರಾಣ is worn down to nothing by too much work; ಕಳವಳ, over in ಭಯ, is being swamped by dread.
    - ಚಡಪಡಿಕೆ | caḍapaḍike | Restless
      note: Onomatopoeic — the sound of a fish on dry ground, or a body that cannot stay in the chair.
  - ದಣಿವು | daṇivu | Tired
    also: ಆಯಾಸ | āyāsa | fatigue ;; ಬಳಲಿಕೆ | baḷalike | exhaustion ;; ಸುಸ್ತು | sustu | done in
    note: The only match rala had for <i>tired</i> was agronomy — ದಣಿದ ಮಣ್ಣು, exhausted soil. ದಣಿವು is used of a body and of a day alike.
    - ತೂಕಡಿಕೆ | tūkaḍike | Sleepy
      also: ಜೋಂಪು | jōmpu | the afternoon drowse ;; ಮಂಪರು | mamparu | half-sleep ;; ನಿದ್ದೆ | nidde | sleep
      note: The nod of the head as you lose the fight.
    - ಅನ್ಯಮನಸ್ಕ | anya-manaska | Unfocused
      note: 'Other-minded' — your mind is somewhere, just not here. Kinder than 'distracted', which implies something pulled you.
- ಭಯ | bhaya | Fearful
  also: ಹೆದರಿಕೆ | hedarike | being scared ;; ಅಂಜಿಕೆ | añjike | timidity ;; ಭೀತಿ | bhīti | terror ;; ದಿಗಿಲು | digilu | dread ;; ಗಾಬರಿ | gābari | panic ;; ಆತಂಕ | ātaṅka | anxiety
  note: rala's richest sector — seven distinct words on the first page. Kannada grades fear finely: ಅಂಜಿಕೆ (timid), ಹೆದರಿಕೆ (scared), ದಿಗಿಲು (dread), ಗಾಬರಿ (panic), ಆತಂಕ (anxiety), ಭೀತಿ (terror).
  - ಹೆದರಿಕೆ | hedarike | Scared
    - ಅಸಹಾಯಕತೆ | asahāyakate | Helpless
      note: rala's ದಿಕ್ಕಿಲ್ಲದ is better than the headword: 'without a direction'. Helplessness as having nowhere to turn — literally no compass point.
    - ಅಂಜಿಕೆ | añjike | Frightened
  - ಆತಂಕ | ātaṅka | Anxious
    also: ಕಳವಳ | kaḷavaḷa | agitation ;; ಚಿಂತೆ | cinte | worry, thought ;; ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ವ್ಯಾಕುಲ | vyākula | distress
    note: ಆತಂಕ is now the standard clinical word too. Its older sense is closer to 'impediment' — anxiety as the thing in your way.
    - ಚಿಂತೆ | cinte | Worried
      also: ಯೋಚನೆ | yōcane | thinking it over ;; ಕಾಳಜಿ | kāḷaji | care, concern ;; ತಲೆಬಿಸಿ | talebisi | head-heat
      note: ಚಿಂತೆ is also simply 'thought'. To worry and to think are the same verb, which tells you something.
    - ಕಳವಳ | kaḷavaḷa | Overwhelmed
      note: The second of the split — see ಹೈರಾಣ under ಬೇಸರ. ಕಳವಳ is being flooded by apprehension rather than by workload.
  - ಅಳುಕು | aḷuku | Insecure
    also: ಅಭದ್ರತೆ | abhadrate | insecurity, of a thing unguarded ;; ಹಿಂಜರಿಕೆ | hiñjarike | hesitation ;; ಶಂಕೆ | śaṅke | misgiving with fear in it
    note: The small inward flinch before you do the thing anyway. ಅಭದ್ರತೆ carries the other sense of <i>insecure</i> — physically unguarded, a word for buildings and borders.
    - ಕೊರತೆ | korate | Inadequate
      note: ಕೊರತೆ is a shortfall — of rain, of funds, of oneself. The same word, which quietly makes it feel less like a personal verdict.
    - ಕೀಳರಿಮೆ | kīḷarime | Inferior
      note: rala gives only the judgement (ಕೀಳು, low-grade). ಕೀಳರಿಮೆ is the feeling — 'low-self-knowing', the exact and rather beautiful Kannada for an inferiority complex.
  - ದುರ್ಬಲ | durbala | Weak
    - ದಂಡ | daṇḍa | Worthless
      also: ನಿಷ್ಪ್ರಯೋಜಕ | niṣprayōjaka | of no use ;; ಅಯೋಗ್ಯ | ayōgya | unworthy ;; ವ್ಯರ್ಥ | vyartha | in vain
      note: Literally waste. 'ನಾನು ದಂಡ' is how the feeling gets said about oneself.
    - ಲೆಕ್ಕಕ್ಕಿಲ್ಲ | lekkakkilla | Insignificant
      also: ಕ್ಷುಲ್ಲಕ | kṣullaka | trivial ;; ಅಲ್ಪ | alpa | slight ;; ನಿಕೃಷ್ಟ | nikṛṣṭa | abject
      note: 'Not in the count.' Insignificance as an accounting error.
  - ತಿರಸ್ಕಾರ | tiraskāra | Rejected
    note: ತಿರಸ್ಕಾರ is what the other person did. As with ಒಪ್ಪಿಗೆ, Kannada gives you no noun for the receiving end — rejection is only ever described from outside.
    - ಹೊರಗಿಡು | horagiḍu | Excluded
      also: ಬಹಿಷ್ಕಾರ | bahiṣkāra | boycott, outcasting ;; ದೂರವಿಡು | dūraviḍu | to keep at a distance ;; ಪ್ರತ್ಯೇಕಿಸು | pratyēkisu | to separate off
      note: Kannada says it as something done to you — ಹೊರಗಿಟ್ಟರು, they kept me out. ಬಹಿಷ್ಕಾರ is the same act with a history attached to it.
    - ಕಿರುಕುಳ | kirukuḷa | Persecuted
  - ಬೆದರಿಕೆ | bedarike | Threatened
    also: ಹೆದರಿಕೆ | hedarike | being scared ;; ಬೆಚ್ಚು | beccu | a startle ;; ಅಪಾಯ | apāya | danger
    note: No entry for the adjective. ಬೆದರಿಕೆ is the threat itself; feeling threatened is said as ಬೆದರಿಕೆ ಇದೆ — 'there is a threat' — placing it outside you rather than inside.
    - ನಡುಕ | naḍuka | Nervous
      note: Most hits were neuroanatomy. ನಡುಕ is the tremble itself — Kannada again naming the body and letting the feeling follow.
    - ಬಟಾಬಯಲು | baṭā-bayalu | Exposed
      note: ಬಟಾಬಯಲು is open ground with not one thing to hide behind — used for landscape and for people, with no change of tone.
- ಕೋಪ | kōpa | Angry
  also: ಸಿಟ್ಟು | siṭṭu | hot, quick anger ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ರೋಷ | rōṣa | fury ;; ಸಿಡುಕು | siḍuku | worn-in irritability ;; ಮುನಿಸು | munisu | the loving sulk ;; ತಾಪ | tāpa | heat
  note: Kannada separates anger by heat and by intimacy: ಸಿಟ್ಟು is hot and quick, ಕೋಪ is the general word, ಕ್ರೋಧ is grand and destructive, ಸಿಡುಕು is chronic and worn on the face — and ಮುನಿಸು is the anger you only get to have with someone who loves you.
  - ಕೈಕೊಟ್ಟರು | kai-koṭṭaru | Let down
    note: rala's single match for <i>let down</i> is the dairy term for milk ejection. Kannada has no noun here either — you say ಕೈಕೊಟ್ಟರು, 'they gave me the hand', meaning they withdrew it at the moment you leaned on it.
    - ದ್ರೋಹ | drōha | Betrayed
      note: ದ್ರೋಹ is grave — the word used for treason and for betraying a guru. Kannada does not have a casual register for this.
    - ಅಸಮಾಧಾನ | asamādhāna | Resentful
      note: Literally 'un-settledness' — the negation of ಸಮಾಧಾನ, consolation. Resentment as a grievance that was never talked down.
  - ಅವಮಾನ | avamāna | Humiliated
    note: rala's ತೇಜೋವಧೆ is worth keeping: 'the murder of someone's lustre'. Humiliation as an assassination of light.
    - ಅವಮರ್ಯಾದೆ | avamaryāde | Disrespected
      also: ಅಗೌರವ | agaurava | disrespect ;; ಉಪೇಕ್ಷೆ | upēkṣe | being overlooked ;; ತಿರಸ್ಕಾರ | tiraskāra | rejection, contempt
      note: ಮರ್ಯಾದೆ — the respect owed to you in public — is one of the most-used words in Kannada. This is its negation.
    - ಗೇಲಿ | gēli | Ridiculed
      also: ಅಪಹಾಸ್ಯ | apahāsya | ridicule ;; ಅಣಕ | aṇaka | mimicry ;; ಅವಹೇಳನ | avahēḷana | belittling ;; ಕುಚೋದ್ಯ | kucōdya | malicious teasing
      note: Laughter turned and pointed. The wound is that a good thing was aimed at you.
  - ಕಹಿ | kahi | Bitter
    note: rala gives mostly vegetables. But the metaphor is alive in Kannada too — ಮನಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕಹಿ, bitterness in the mind — so the taste-word earns its place here on its own terms, not as a calque.
    - ಆಕ್ರೋಶ | ākrōśa | Indignant
      note: rala's words are plain anger. ಆಕ್ರೋಶ is anger with a case to argue — literally an outcry, the anger of protest.
    - ಭಂಗ | bhaṅga | Violated
      note: A real hole. rala's options are either legal (breaking a rule) or the specific term for sexual assault. There is no neutral Kannada for 'I feel violated' — the therapeutic middle register simply hasn't been built yet.
  - ಸಿಟ್ಟು | siṭṭu | Mad
    also: ಕೋಪ | kōpa | anger, composed ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ಮುನಿಸು | munisu | the loving sulk ;; ಸೆಡವು | seḍavu | a huff
    note: Hot and quick, and the most-used of the anger words.
    - ರೊಚ್ಚು | roccu | Furious
      also: ರೋಷ | rōṣa | fury ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ಆವೇಶ | āvēśa | frenzy, possession ;; ಉಗ್ರ | ugra | ferocious
      note: ರೊಚ್ಚಿಗೇಳು — to rise into ರೊಚ್ಚು. Anger that has got into the body and lifted you out of your seat.
    - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಕಿಚ್ಚು | hoṭṭe-kiccu | Jealous
      also: ಅಸೂಯೆ | asūye | envy ;; ಮಾತ್ಸರ್ಯ | mātsarya | envious rivalry ;; ಕರುಬು | karubu | to begrudge, to smoulder ;; ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯುರಿ | hoṭṭeyuri | the burn of being wronged
      note: Belly-fire. Envy located precisely in the stomach, and said out loud — everyone knows which organ is burning.
  - ಜಗಳಗಂಟ | jagaḷagaṇṭa | Aggressive
    also: ಆಕ್ರಮಣಶೀಲ | ākramaṇaśīla | aggressive ;; ಕಾದಾಟ | kādāṭa | fighting ;; ಹಟಮಾರಿ | haṭamāri | obstinate and combative
    note: 'Quarrel-knot' — a person who ties fights. rala offered this one itself.
    - ಕೆರಳಿಕೆ | keraḷike | Provoked
      note: ಕೆಣಕು is the good one: to poke a thing that was sitting quietly.
    - ಹಗೆತನ | hagetana | Hostile
      note: ಹಗೆ is the old native word for enemy, and it is heavy — the enmity of feuds and epics, not of office politics.
  - ರೇಜಿಗೆ | rējige | Frustrated
    note: rala reads <i>frustrate</i> as 'to thwart' — an outcome. Frustration as an ongoing state is ರೇಜಿಗೆ: exasperation at something that keeps not working.
    - ಕೆಂಡಾಮಂಡಲ | keṇḍā-maṇḍala | Infuriated
      note: 'A whole mandala of live coals.' One of the finest anger words in the language, and unfindable from English.
    - ಕಿರಿಕಿರಿ | kirikiri | Annoyed
      note: Onomatopoeia again — the sound of a small grating thing. Kannada builds its minor irritations out of noise.
  - ಬಿಗುಮಾನ | bigumāna | Distant
    note: rala found it exactly. ಬಿಗುಮಾನ is stiffness held on purpose — reserve that is also a kind of self-regard. And note its opposite in rala's own list: ಸಲಿಗೆ ಇಲ್ಲದ, 'without ಸಲಿಗೆ'.
    - ಮುದುಡು | muduḍu | Withdrawn
      note: rala only knows withdrawn tenders. ಮುದುಡು is what a leaf or a touched mimosa does — to fold inward. Exactly right for a person.
    - ಮರಗಟ್ಟು | maragaṭṭu | Numb
      note: ಮರ + ಕಟ್ಟು: to turn to wood. Used for a foot that has gone to sleep and for a grief that has stopped registering.
  - ಟೀಕೆ | ṭīke | Critical
    note: Physics and statistics, mostly. ಟೀಕೆ is fault-finding; ವಿಮರ್ಶೆ, also in the list, is the honourable kind — literary criticism. Kannada distinguishes the two, English does not.
    - ಅನುಮಾನ | anumāna | Skeptical
      note: Four graded words for doubt. ಶಂಕೆ leans toward fear, ಸಂಶಯ toward suspicion of a person, ಸಂದೇಹ toward uncertainty about a fact.
    - ಉಡಾಫೆ | uḍāphe | Dismissive
      note: ಉಡಾಫೆ is dismissiveness worn as a style — breezy, unbothered, faintly insulting. ಅಸಡ್ಡೆ and ತಾತ್ಸಾರ are the colder cousins.
- ಅಸಹ್ಯ | asahya | Disgusted
  also: ಜಿಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jigupse | world-weary revulsion ;; ಜುಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jugupse | the same, other spelling ;; ಹೇಸಿಗೆ | hēsige | filth, loathing ;; ರೋಸು | rōsu | fed up to nausea ;; ವಾಕರಿಕೆ | vākarike | nausea
  note: ಅಸಹ್ಯ literally means 'unbearable' — what cannot be borne. Kannada files disgust under endurance rather than under taste.
  - ಒಪ್ಪದಿರು | oppadiru | Disapproving
    also: ಅಸಮ್ಮತಿ | asammati | dissent ;; ಮೆಚ್ಚದಿರು | meccadiru | to not approve ;; ಆಕ್ಷೇಪ | ākṣēpa | objection
    note: Kannada's ordinary form here is the plain negative verb: they did not agree.
    - ಕೊಂಕು | koṅku | Judgmental
      note: rala went straight to the judiciary. ಕೊಂಕು is the crooked remark — fault-finding delivered sideways, which is how it usually arrives.
    - ಮುಜುಗರ | mujugara | Embarrassed
      also: ಸಂಕೋಚ | saṅkōca | shrinking ;; ಇರುಸುಮುರುಸು | irusumurusu | squirming discomfort ;; ಕಸಿವಿಸಿ | kasivisi | small unease
      note: ಮುಜುಗರ is social awkwardness — the wince at a scene, often on someone else's behalf.
  - ನಿರಾಸೆ | nirāse | Disappointed
    note: ನಿರಾಸೆ = ನಿರ್ + ಆಸೆ, de-hoped. ಆಶಾಭಂಗ, also offered, is stronger: hope actually broken.
    - ಹೌಹಾರು | hauhāru | Appalled
      also: ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ | digbhrame | stupefaction ;; ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು | beccibīḷu | to be startled and drop ;; ಗಾಬರಿ | gābari | panic
      note: Onomatopoeic — to recoil bodily on hearing something.
    - ರೋಸು | rōsu | Revolted
      note: rala took <i>revolt</i> politically — every hit is an uprising. ರೋಸಿಹೋಗಿದೆ is the feeling: fed up to the point of nausea.
  - ಘೋರ | ghōra | Awful
    also: ಅಸಹನೀಯ | asahanīya | unbearable ;; ಭೀಕರ | bhīkara | dire ;; ಕೆಟ್ಟ | keṭṭa | bad, of quality or morals
    note: Used for an accident and for a cricket collapse alike.
    - ವಾಕರಿಕೆ | vākarike | Nauseated
      note: ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ ತೊಳಸು — 'the stomach stirs'. Kannada has a full vocabulary for the gut, and uses it for feelings without apology.
    - ಹೇಸಿಗೆ | hēsige | Detestable
      also: ಅಸಹ್ಯ | asahya | disgust ;; ಕೊಳಕು | koḷaku | dirt ;; ಜುಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jugupse | revulsion
      note: ಹೇಸಿಗೆ is also literally filth. The moral and the physical are the same word — no metaphor required.
  - ಜಿಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jigupse | Repelled
    note: ಜಿಗುಪ್ಸೆ is world-weary revulsion — the disgust that makes people renounce things, not just push a plate away.
    - ದಿಗಿಲು | digilu | Horrified
      also: ಆತಂಕ | ātaṅka | anxiety ;; ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ಭೀತಿ | bhīti | terror
    - ಹಿಂಜರಿಕೆ | hiñjarike | Hesitant
      note: ಹಿಂಜರಿ — to slide backwards. The foot that starts to move and then doesn't.
- ದುಃಖ | duḥkha | Sad
  also: ಶೋಕ | śōka | formal mourning ;; ವ್ಯಥೆ | vyathe | affliction ;; ಸಂಕಟ | saṅkaṭa | the chest closing ;; ಕೊರಗು | koragu | the grief that thins you ;; ವಿಷಾದ | viṣāda | melancholy ;; ಅಳಲು | aḷalu | the wail
  note: Note ಅಶುಭ / ಅಮಂಗಳ in rala's list — 'inauspicious'. For a large part of Kannada usage, sadness and bad omen are adjacent ideas; a sad event is an unlucky one.
  - ಒಂಟಿತನ | oṇṭitana | Lonely
    also: ಏಕಾಂತ | ēkānta | solitude, chosen and good ;; ಒಬ್ಬಂಟಿ | obbaṇṭi | all by oneself ;; ನಿರ್ಜನ | nirjana | deserted
    note: Kannada draws a line English blurs: ಒಂಟಿತನ is loneliness and it hurts; ಏಕಾಂತ is solitude, chosen, and is good for you. Same 'alone', opposite verdicts.
    - ಏಕಾಂಗಿ | ēkāṅgi | Isolated
      also: ಒಂಟಿ | oṇṭi | alone ;; ದಿಕ್ಕಿಲ್ಲದ | dikkillada | without a direction to turn ;; ಅನಾಥ | anātha | without protector
      note: 'Single-bodied' — cut off with no one on your side.
    - ತಬ್ಬಲಿ | tabbali | Abandoned
      note: ತಬ್ಬಲಿ means orphan, and it is used far past its literal sense — for anyone left without their people. One of the saddest words in the language. Orphan — used far past its literal sense, for anyone left without their people. One of the saddest words in the language.
  - ದುರ್ಬಲತೆ | durbalate | Vulnerable
    note: The clearest gap on the wheel. Every Kannada option means weak, breachable, at risk — all pejorative. The warm English sense of 'vulnerable', where opening up is a strength, has no Kannada word yet; people say ಮನಸ್ಸು ತೆರೆದಿಡುವುದು, 'to keep the mind open', as a description rather than a name.
    - ಬಲಿಪಶು | balipaśu | Victimised
      note: 'Sacrificial animal'. Kannada's word for victim comes straight off the altar.
    - ನಾಜೂಕು | nājūku | Fragile
      note: ನಾಜೂಕು is fragile-and-fine, a compliment about a person's delicacy. ಭಂಗುರ is the philosophical one: that which is destined to break.
  - ಹತಾಶೆ | hatāśe | Despair
    note: ಹತ + ಆಶೆ: hope, killed. The word contains the murder.
    - ಅಳಲು | aḷalu | Grief
      also: ಶೋಕ | śōka | mourning ;; ಗೋಳು | gōḷu | wretched crying ;; ರೋದನ | rōdana | lamentation
      note: rala's whole list is worth reading: ಶೋಕ is formal mourning, ಸಂಕಟ is the chest-squeeze, ಕೊರಗು is the grief that thins you over years, ಅಳಲು is the wail itself.
    - ಕೈಲಾಗದು | kailāgadu | Powerless
      note: rala offers strength-less. ಕೈಲಾಗದತನ is the spoken form: 'the state of the hands not managing it'.
  - ಪಾಪಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ | pāpa-prajñe | Guilty
    note: rala's hits for <i>guilty</i> are all courtroom Kannada — the accused, the convicted. ಪಾಪಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ, sin-consciousness, comes from the other direction: Kannada's guilt is borrowed either from law or from the temple.
    - ನಾಚಿಕೆ | nāchike | Ashamed
      also: ಸಂಕೋಚ | saṅkōca | shrinking, reticence ;; ಮುಜುಗರ | mujugara | awkwardness ;; ಲಜ್ಜೆ | lajje | modesty, shame
      note: ನಾಚಿಕೆ is one word for shyness, modesty and shame — a bride's ನಾಚಿಕೆ and a thief's are the same noun. English needs three words and grades them differently; Kannada trusts context completely.
    - ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ | paścāttāpa | Remorseful
      note: 'After-heat' — the burn that arrives once the act is over.
  - ಖಿನ್ನತೆ | khinnate | Depressed
    note: rala's first hits for <i>depressed</i> are ದಲಿತ and ಶೋಷಿತ — from the colonial administrative phrase 'depressed classes'. ಖಿನ್ನತೆ is the clinical word; ಮನಸ್ಸು ಕುಗ್ಗಿದೆ, the mind has shrunk, is the older way of saying it.
    - ಕುಗ್ಗು | kuggu | Inferior
      note: The English wheel repeats <i>inferior</i> in two branches. Kannada usefully does not: under fear it is ಕೀಳರಿಮೆ, a belief about your rank; here under sadness it is simply shrinking.
    - ಬರಿದು | baridu | Empty
      note: rala's ಪೊಳ್ಳು is the good one — hollow, like a grain with nothing inside it. Used of people who look intact.
  - ನೋವು | nōvu | Hurt
    also: ಬೇನೆ | bēne | ache, ailment ;; ಯಾತನೆ | yātane | torment ;; ಬಾಧೆ | bādhe | affliction
    note: ನೋವು is bodily pain and emotional pain with no distinction at all. 'ಮನಸ್ಸಿಗೆ ನೋವಾಯಿತು' — it hurt my mind — is the ordinary way to say you were wounded.
    - ಆಶಾಭಂಗ | āśābhaṅga | Disappointed
      note: The second 'disappointed' on the wheel. ನಿರಾಸೆ over in ಅಸಹ್ಯ is hope that faded; ಆಶಾಭಂಗ is hope that snapped.
    - ಸಂಕೋಚ | saṅkōca | Embarrassed
      note: The other 'embarrassed'. ಮುಜುಗರ is the wince at a social scene; ಸಂಕೋಚ is the shrinking-in-on-yourself, the hesitation to ask, to take, to take up room.
