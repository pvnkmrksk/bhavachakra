# ರಸಚಕ್ರ
id: rasa
hub: ನವರಸ | nava-rasa · the nine flavours
tag: The nine rasas, each with its ಸ್ಥಾಯಿಭಾವ, opened out into daily Kannada.

- ಶೃಂಗಾರ | śṛṅgāra | love, the erotic
  sthayi: ರತಿ · rati, desire
  note: The first and most argued-over rasa. It is not romance in the modern sense — ಶೃಂಗಾರ covers the whole apparatus of attraction, adornment and separation, and half of it is about being apart.
  - ಒಲವು | olavu | fondness, leaning-toward
    note: Native, and the gentlest of the love words. You have ಒಲವು for a person, a place, or an idea.
    - ಪ್ರೀತಿ | prīti | love
      also: ಮಮತೆ | mamate | attachment-love ;; ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ | vātsalya | tenderness flowing downward ;; ಅಕ್ಕರೆ | akkare | fondness ;; ಒಲವು | olavu | leaning toward
      note: The general word, used for parents, friends and lovers without embarrassment.
    - ಮೆಚ್ಚುಗೆ | meccuge | liking, approval
      also: ಪ್ರಶಂಸೆ | praśaṁse | praise ;; ಒಲವು | olavu | leaning toward ;; ಇಷ್ಟ | iṣṭa | liking
      note: Both the feeling and its expression — to ಮೆಚ್ಚು silently is incomplete.
  - ಸರಸ | sarasa | playful flirtation
    note: Literally 'with rasa'. The word for banter between people who like each other, and it is not coy about it.
    - ಚೆಲ್ಲಾಟ | cellāṭa | dalliance
      note: Play with a loose edge to it — used affectionately and as a mild accusation.
    - ನಾಚಿಕೆ | nāchike | bashfulness
      also: ಸಂಕೋಚ | saṅkōca | shrinking, reticence ;; ಮುಜುಗರ | mujugara | awkwardness ;; ಲಜ್ಜೆ | lajje | modesty, shame
      note: Shyness, modesty and shame in one noun. A bride's ನಾಚಿಕೆ and a thief's are the same word.
  - ವಿರಹ | viraha | the pain of separation
    note: A whole genre of Kannada poetry sits here. English has no single word, which is why so much of it gets translated as 'longing' and loses the ache.
    - ಹಂಬಲ | hambala | yearning
      also: ಕಾತರ | kātara | aching eagerness ;; ತವಕ | tavaka | eagerness ;; ಹಪಹಪಿ | hapahapi | craving
      note: The pull toward something absent — a place, a person, a life not lived.
    - ಕಾತರ | kātara | aching eagerness
      note: Waiting that has begun to hurt slightly.
  - ಮೋಹ | mōha | infatuation
    note: In philosophy ಮೋಹ is delusion, one of the six enemies. In daily speech it is simply being besotted — the moral warning is still audible underneath.
    - ಸೆಳೆತ | seḷeta | pull, attraction
      note: Native and physical: the same word for an undertow and for a muscle cramp.
    - ವ್ಯಾಮೋಹ | vyāmōha | obsessive attachment
      note: ಮೋಹ with the brakes off. Said of parents about children as often as of lovers.
- ಹಾಸ್ಯ | hāsya | mirth, the comic
  sthayi: ಹಾಸ · hāsa, laughter
  note: The rasa the English feeling wheel has no room for at all. Kannada grades laughter finely, and most of the grades are about who is being laughed at.
  - ನಗು | nagu | laughter
    note: The plain native verb-noun. Everything else in this sector is a shade of it.
    - ನಗೆ | nage | a laugh
      note: The countable one — you can have a ನಗೆ, you cannot have a ನಗು.
    - ಕಿಸಿಕಿಸಿ | kisikisi | giggling
      note: Sound-word. The laughter you are trying and failing to suppress.
  - ತಮಾಷೆ | tamāṣe | fun
    note: Borrowed from Urdu and now completely at home. 'ತಮಾಷೆಗೆ ಹೇಳಿದೆ' — I said it for fun — is the standard retreat.
    - ತುಂಟತನ | ṭuṇṭatana | mischief
      note: The naughtiness of a child you are not actually angry with.
    - ಕೀಟಲೆ | kīṭale | teasing
      note: Teasing you are allowed to do, which means it is a claim about the relationship.
  - ಗೇಲಿ | gēli | mockery
    also: ಅಪಹಾಸ್ಯ | apahāsya | ridicule ;; ಅಣಕ | aṇaka | mimicry ;; ಅವಹೇಳನ | avahēḷana | belittling ;; ಕುಚೋದ್ಯ | kucōdya | malicious teasing
    note: Laughter turned and pointed. The wound is that a good thing was aimed at you.
    - ಅಣಕ | aṇaka | mimicry
      note: Doing an impression of someone to their disadvantage.
    - ವ್ಯಂಗ್ಯ | vyaṅgya | sarcasm
      note: In poetics ವ್ಯಂಗ್ಯ is suggested meaning — the good kind. In an argument it is the knife.
  - ಮುಗುಳ್ನಗೆ | muguḷnage | a smile
    note: 'Bud-laugh' — the laugh that has not opened. A compound of exactly the kind Kannada makes best.
    - ಸಂತಸ | santasa | gladness
      note: Gladness, with a quieter and more inward feel than ಸಂತೋಷ.
    - ಹಗುರ | hagura | lightness
      note: The change in weight after something is resolved.
- ಕರುಣ | karuṇa | compassion, pathos
  sthayi: ಶೋಕ · śōka, grief
  note: The rasa Kannada literature is most at home in. Note that it names not grief itself but grief-made-shareable — the feeling an audience has, not the one the character has.
  - ಅಳಲು | aḷalu | the wail
    also: ಶೋಕ | śōka | mourning ;; ಗೋಳು | gōḷu | wretched crying ;; ರೋದನ | rōdana | lamentation
    note: Native, and it is the sound before it is the feeling.
    - ದುಃಖ | duḥkha | sorrow
      also: ಶೋಕ | śōka | formal mourning ;; ವ್ಯಥೆ | vyathe | affliction ;; ಸಂಕಟ | saṅkaṭa | the chest closing ;; ಕೊರಗು | koragu | the grief that thins you ;; ವಿಷಾದ | viṣāda | melancholy ;; ಅಳಲು | aḷalu | the wail
      note: The general word, and also the technical Buddhist one. Kannada uses it for a bad afternoon.
    - ಕಣ್ಣೀರು | kaṇṇīru | tears
      note: Eye-water. Plain compound, no ceremony — Kannada saves the ceremony for the phrases around it.
  - ಕನಿಕರ | kanikara | compassion
    also: ಅನುಕಂಪ | anukampa | fellow-feeling ;; ಮರುಕ | maruka | pity, ruth ;; ಕರುಣೆ | karuṇe | mercy ;; ದಯೆ | daye | kindness
    note: What you feel toward someone whose situation you can see clearly. Not quite pity — there is less height in it.
    - ಮರುಕ | maruka | pity, ruth
      note: 'Turning back toward' — pity as the movement of turning to look again at someone.
    - ಕರುಳು ಚುರುಕ್ | karuḷu curuk | the gut-pang
      note: The involuntary sting on seeing a child or an animal in distress. Pity is a judgement; this is a reflex.
  - ಸಂಕಟ | saṅkaṭa | anguish
    also: ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ಒದ್ದಾಟ | oddāṭa | thrashing about ;; ಪರದಾಟ | paradāṭa | floundering
    note: Felt as constriction — the chest closing. Used equally for a dying person's distress and for an impossible choice.
    - ಉಮ್ಮಳ | ummaḷa | grief welling up
      note: Names the swell, not the weeping — the moment before it breaks.
    - ಕೊರಗು | koragu | pining
      note: The grief that thins you over years rather than days.
  - ಹಳಹಳಿಕೆ | haḷahaḷike | regret braided with longing
    note: Remorse for something you would, honestly, do again. English has to use a whole sentence.
    - ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ | paścāttāpa | remorse
      note: 'After-heat' — the burn that arrives once the act is over.
    - ವಿಷಾದ | viṣāda | melancholy
      note: Sadness with the edges worn off. The word Kannada writers reach for when nothing in particular is wrong.
- ರೌದ್ರ | raudra | fury
  sthayi: ಕ್ರೋಧ · krōdha, wrath
  note: Named for Rudra. The rasa is deliberately grand — a god's anger, not a bad mood — which is why the daily words underneath it feel so much smaller and so much more used.
  - ಸಿಟ್ಟು | siṭṭu | anger
    also: ಕೋಪ | kōpa | anger, composed ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ಮುನಿಸು | munisu | the loving sulk ;; ಸೆಡವು | seḍavu | a huff
    note: Hot and quick, and the most-used of the anger words.
    - ಕೋಪ | kōpa | anger, the general word
      also: ಸಿಟ್ಟು | siṭṭu | hot, quick anger ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ರೋಷ | rōṣa | fury ;; ಸಿಡುಕು | siḍuku | worn-in irritability ;; ಮುನಿಸು | munisu | the loving sulk ;; ತಾಪ | tāpa | heat
      note: Slightly more composed than ಸಿಟ್ಟು — you can have ಕೋಪ quietly.
    - ಸಿಡುಕು | siḍuku | irritability
      note: Not an episode but a temperament, and one you wear on your face.
  - ರೊಚ್ಚು | roccu | rage
    also: ರೋಷ | rōṣa | fury ;; ಕ್ರೋಧ | krōdha | wrath ;; ಆವೇಶ | āvēśa | frenzy, possession ;; ಉಗ್ರ | ugra | ferocious
    note: ರೊಚ್ಚಿಗೇಳು — to rise into ರೊಚ್ಚು. Anger that has got into the body and lifted you out of your seat.
    - ಕೆಂಡಾಮಂಡಲ | keṇḍā-maṇḍala | incandescent
      note: 'A whole mandala of live coals.' One of the finest anger words in the language.
    - ಆವೇಶ | āvēśa | frenzy
      note: Also the word for being possessed by a deity. The grammar says the feeling is driving, not you.
  - ಹಗೆ | hage | enmity
    note: The old native word for an enemy, and heavy — the enmity of feuds, not of office politics.
    - ದ್ವೇಷ | dvēṣa | hatred
      note: Settled and directed. One of the six enemies in the moral vocabulary.
    - ಸೇಡು | sēḍu | revenge
      note: Native. ಸೇಡು ತೀರಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳು — to settle the revenge — treats it as a debt.
  - ಆಕ್ರೋಶ | ākrōśa | outcry
    note: Anger with a case to argue. The word every Kannada news bulletin uses for public anger.
    - ಅಸಮಾಧಾನ | asamādhāna | discontent
      note: 'Un-settledness' — the negation of consolation. A grievance nobody talked down.
    - ಬಂಡಾಯ | baṇḍāya | revolt
      note: Also the name of a Kannada literary movement, which is the right amount of baggage.
- ವೀರ | vīra | the heroic
  sthayi: ಉತ್ಸಾಹ · utsāha, vigour
  note: The rasa with no home on the English wheel at all. Its ಸ್ಥಾಯಿಭಾವ is not courage but ಉತ್ಸಾಹ — energy — which quietly claims that heroism is a kind of enthusiasm.
  - ಧೈರ್ಯ | dhairya | courage
    also: ಕೆಚ್ಚು | keccu | embers-courage ;; ಎದೆಗಾರಿಕೆ | edegārike | nerve ;; ದಿಟ್ಟತನ | diṭṭatana | boldness ;; ಛಲ | chala | resolve
    note: Steadiness under fear. The everyday word, and the one you tell someone to have.
    - ಕೆಚ್ಚು | keccu | fierce courage
      note: Heat held in the body. Kannada's bravery word is thermal, not moral.
    - ಎದೆಗಾರಿಕೆ | edegārike | nerve
      note: 'Chest-having' — the willingness to stand up and say it.
  - ಛಲ | chala | resolve
    note: One of the most-used words in Kannada self-description. Not stubbornness — the refusal to be finished with something.
    - ಹಠ | haṭha | insistence
      note: ಛಲ's difficult sibling. A child throwing ಹಠ and a satyagrahi holding it are the same noun.
    - ಪಟ್ಟು | paṭṭu | a hold, a grip
      note: From wrestling. ಪಟ್ಟು ಬಿಡದೆ — without letting go of the hold — is how persistence is described.
  - ಹೆಮ್ಮೆ | hemme | pride
    also: ಅಭಿಮಾನ | abhimāna | pride as loyalty ;; ಗರ್ವ | garva | pride, tipping toward vanity ;; ಅಹಂಕಾರ | ahaṅkāra | the pride that has gone bad ;; ಗತ್ತು | gattu | swagger
    note: Warm pride, usually in someone else. Distinct from ಅಹಂಕಾರ, which is the pride that has gone bad.
    - ಅಭಿಮಾನ | abhimāna | pride-as-loyalty
      note: For your language, your team, your people. Its second sense is the wound when that loyalty is slighted.
    - ಗತ್ತು | gattu | swagger
      note: Native, and affectionately used — the carriage of someone who knows they are good.
  - ಹುರುಪು | hurupu | vigour
    note: Native. The energy you start a thing with, before ಛಲ has to take over.
    - ಉಮೇದು | umēdu | zest
      note: Borrowed and thoroughly domesticated. 'ಉಮೇದು ಇಲ್ಲ' is a complete diagnosis.
    - ಸಂಭ್ರಮ | sambhrama | festive elation
      note: Busy, shared, slightly frantic joy — a wedding house at six in the morning. Nobody has ಸಂಭ್ರಮ alone.
- ಭಯಾನಕ | bhayānaka | terror
  sthayi: ಭಯ · bhaya, fear
  note: Kannada's richest sector by count. Fear is graded by intensity and by whether you can see it coming.
  - ಹೆದರಿಕೆ | hedarike | being scared
    note: The plain daily word, and the one children are told not to have.
    - ಅಂಜಿಕೆ | añjike | timidity
      note: Fear as a disposition. ಅಂಜುಬುರುಕ, one who is full of it, is a mild insult.
    - ಬೆದರಿಕೆ | bedarike | a threat
      also: ಹೆದರಿಕೆ | hedarike | being scared ;; ಬೆಚ್ಚು | beccu | a startle ;; ಅಪಾಯ | apāya | danger
      note: The threat itself. Kannada places it outside you — 'ಬೆದರಿಕೆ ಇದೆ', there is a threat.
  - ದಿಗಿಲು | digilu | dread
    also: ಆತಂಕ | ātaṅka | anxiety ;; ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ಭೀತಿ | bhīti | terror
    note: Heavier and more still than ಹೆದರಿಕೆ. The fear that has settled in and is waiting.
    - ಆತಂಕ | ātaṅka | anxiety
      also: ಕಳವಳ | kaḷavaḷa | agitation ;; ಚಿಂತೆ | cinte | worry, thought ;; ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn ;; ವ್ಯಾಕುಲ | vyākula | distress
      note: Now the standard clinical word too. Its older sense is closer to 'impediment' — anxiety as the thing in your way.
    - ತಳಮಳ | taḷamaḷa | churn
      note: The word for boiling liquid and for a mind that will not settle.
  - ಗಾಬರಿ | gābari | panic
    note: Sudden, visible, and slightly undignified — the fear other people can see you having.
    - ಬೆಚ್ಚು | beccu | a startle
      note: ಬೆಚ್ಚಿಬೀಳು — to be startled and drop. Kannada builds the flinch out of a fall.
    - ನಡುಕ | naḍuka | the tremble
      note: The body named, the feeling left to be inferred.
  - ಅಳುಕು | aḷuku | misgiving
    also: ಅಭದ್ರತೆ | abhadrate | insecurity, of a thing unguarded ;; ಹಿಂಜರಿಕೆ | hiñjarike | hesitation ;; ಶಂಕೆ | śaṅke | misgiving with fear in it
    note: The small inward flinch before you do the thing anyway. ಅಭದ್ರತೆ carries the other sense of <i>insecure</i> — physically unguarded, a word for buildings and borders.
    - ಹಿಂಜರಿಕೆ | hiñjarike | hesitation
      note: To slide backwards — the foot that starts to move and then does not.
    - ಅನುಮಾನ | anumāna | doubt
      note: One of four graded doubt words. ಶಂಕೆ leans to fear, ಸಂಶಯ to suspicion of a person, ಸಂದೇಹ to uncertainty about a fact.
- ಬೀಭತ್ಸ | bībhatsa | disgust, the odious
  sthayi: ಜುಗುಪ್ಸೆ · jugupse, revulsion
  note: The rasa nobody wants and every tradition keeps. Kannada files disgust under endurance — ಅಸಹ್ಯ literally means what cannot be borne.
  - ಅಸಹ್ಯ | asahya | disgust
    also: ಜಿಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jigupse | world-weary revulsion ;; ಜುಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jugupse | the same, other spelling ;; ಹೇಸಿಗೆ | hēsige | filth, loathing ;; ರೋಸು | rōsu | fed up to nausea ;; ವಾಕರಿಕೆ | vākarike | nausea
    note: 'Unbearable.' The daily word, used for a smell and for a politician with equal ease.
    - ಹೇಸಿಗೆ | hēsige | filth, loathing
      also: ಅಸಹ್ಯ | asahya | disgust ;; ಕೊಳಕು | koḷaku | dirt ;; ಜುಗುಪ್ಸೆ | jugupse | revulsion
      note: Also literally filth. The moral and the physical are one word — no metaphor required.
    - ಕೊಳಕು | koḷaku | dirt
      note: Native and blunt. Calling a person ಕೊಳಕು is not a comment on hygiene.
  - ವಾಕರಿಕೆ | vākarike | nausea
    note: The bodily end of the sector, and Kannada moves between it and the moral end without a signal.
    - ಓಕರಿಕೆ | ōkarike | retching
      note: Onomatopoeic and unglamorous.
    - ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ ತೊಳಸು | hoṭṭe toḷasu | the stomach stirs
      note: Used for moral revulsion and actual nausea with nothing between the two senses.
  - ರೋಸು | rōsu | being fed up
    note: ರೋಸಿಹೋಗಿದೆ — fed up to the point of nausea. The most useful word in this sector.
    - ಬೇಸರ | bēsara | weary discontent
      also: ಬೇಜಾರು | bējāru | fed up, the same word one register down ;; ಸಪ್ಪೆ | sappe | flat, unsalted ;; ಜಡ | jaḍa | inert
      note: One word for bored, mildly sad, and fed up. The listener reads your face for which.
    - ರೇಜಿಗೆ | rējige | exasperation
      note: Disgust at something that keeps not working, rather than at something foul.
  - ತಾತ್ಸಾರ | tātsāra | disdain
    note: Disgust cooled into a social posture — the version you can hold at a wedding.
    - ಅಸಡ್ಡೆ | asaḍḍe | not caring, coldly
      also: ನಿರಾಸಕ್ತಿ | nirāsakti | disinterest ;; ಉದಾಸೀನ | udāsīna | indifference, or the cold shoulder ;; ತಾತ್ಸಾರ | tātsāra | disdain
      note: Not caring, and not quite bothering to hide that you are not caring.
    - ಕೊಂಕು | koṅku | the crooked remark
      note: Fault-finding delivered sideways, which is how it usually arrives.
- ಅದ್ಭುತ | adbhuta | wonder
  sthayi: ವಿಸ್ಮಯ · vismaya, astonishment
  note: The rasa that most resembles a modern emotion. Kannada keeps a native word for it — ಬೆರಗು — which is rarer than it should be.
  - ಬೆರಗು | beragu | amazement
    also: ವಿಸ್ಮಯ | vismaya | astonishment ;; ಅಚ್ಚರಿ | accari | surprise ;; ಸೋಜಿಗ | sōjiga | marvel
    note: Native. Wonder that stops you where you are.
    - ಅಚ್ಚರಿ | accari | surprise
      also: ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ | āścarya | surprise, the Sanskrit form ;; ವಿಸ್ಮಯ | vismaya | astonishment ;; ಬೆರಗು | beragu | wonder that stops you
      note: ಅಚ್ಚರಿ is the native word and ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ the Sanskrit one; both are in daily use, and this wheel uses the shorter.
    - ದಂಗು | daṅgu | dumbfounded
      also: ವಿಸ್ಮಯ | vismaya | astonishment ;; ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ | āścarya | surprise ;; ಬೆರಗು | beragu | wonder ;; ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ | digbhrame | stupefaction
      note: ದಂಗಾದೆ — I was floored. The version of astonishment with your mouth open.
  - ಕುತೂಹಲ | kutūhala | curiosity
    note: Wonder that has turned into a question. Kannada treats it as a virtue.
    - ಆಸಕ್ತಿ | āsakti | interest
      note: Literally attachment — interest as a mild binding to a thing.
    - ತವಕ | tavaka | eagerness
      note: Eagerness with an edge of ache in it.
  - ಪುಳಕ | puḷaka | the thrill
    note: Classical poetics counts it as visible evidence of an inner state, which is why it has its own noun.
    - ರೋಮಾಂಚ | rōmāñca | horripilation
      note: 'Hair-motion.' The Sanskrit register of the same event.
    - ಮೈ ಝುಮ್ | mai jhum | the body tingles
      note: The spoken version, and specifically aesthetic — you say it about a raga.
  - ಭಯಭಕ್ತಿ | bhaya-bhakti | awe
    note: Fear-and-devotion, in one compound. Awe as a social posture — how one stands before a deity or a formidable elder.
    - ಗೌರವ | gaurava | respect
      note: Something you give, actively, not something you passively have.
    - ದಿಗ್ಭ್ರಮೆ | digbhrame | stupefaction
      note: 'Directions-confusion' — the compass spins and you cannot tell which way is which.
- ಶಾಂತ | śānta | repose
  sthayi: ಶಮ · śama, quiet
  note: The ninth rasa, added late and argued over for centuries — can the absence of agitation be a flavour? The English wheel files peace under happiness; the rasa tradition insists it is a state of its own.
  - ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ | nemmadi | peace of mind
    also: ಶಾಂತಿ | śānti | peace, as the absence of conflict ;; ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | being consoled ;; ನಿರಾಳ | nirāḷa | unclenched
    note: Sharply distinct from ಶಾಂತಿ, which is peace as the absence of conflict. You can have ಶಾಂತಿ in a house with no ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ in it.
    - ನಿರಾಳ | nirāḷa | unclenched
      note: The breath after the weight comes off. Freedom as a bodily state, not a political one.
    - ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | being consoled
      also: ಸಂತೈಕೆ | santaike | consoling ;; ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ | nemmadi | peace of mind ;; ತಣ್ಣಗಾಗು | taṇṇagāgu | to cool down
      note: Both the comfort someone gives and the state it produces.
  - ತೃಪ್ತಿ | tṛpti | satiety
    also: ಸಂತೃಪ್ತಿ | santṛpti | full satisfaction ;; ತಣಿವು | taṇivu | slaked ;; ಸಮಾಧಾನ | samādhāna | settledness
    note: The feeling after a meal, and after a life. Kannada uses the same word without irony.
    - ತಣಿವು | taṇivu | slaked
      note: Satisfaction as cooling rather than filling — the other half of how Kannada thinks about want.
    - ಸಾರ್ಥಕ | sārthaka | it was worth it
      note: 'Having meaning.' The feeling, where ಯಶಸ್ವಿ is only the outcome.
  - ವೈರಾಗ್ಯ | vairāgya | detachment
    note: Ordinary speech in Kannada, not only monastic — said of anyone who has stopped wanting a thing they used to want.
    - ಉದಾಸೀನ | udāsīna | equanimity, or the cold shoulder
      note: In philosophy the sage's evenness. In an argument, the coldest insult available. Same word.
    - ನಿರ್ಲಿಪ್ತ | nirlipta | unsmeared
      note: Literally not stuck to anything — the lotus-leaf image, worn down into an ordinary adjective.
  - ಮೌನ | mauna | silence
    note: Treated as an action, not an absence. ಮೌನ ವಹಿಸು — to take up silence — is something you do to someone.
    - ಏಕಾಂತ | ēkānta | solitude, chosen
      note: The good kind of alone. Kannada draws the line English blurs: ಒಂಟಿತನ hurts, ಏಕಾಂತ is sought.
    - ತಂಪು | tampu | coolness
      note: Native, and the whole thermal vocabulary in one word — the opposite pole from ಸಿಟ್ಟು, ಕಿಚ್ಚು, ಬಿಸಿ and ಉರಿ.
